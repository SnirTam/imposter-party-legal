Support

Questions, bug reports or deck ideas? Email tamsnir@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Is it free? Yes. The app is free to download, and all 9 games are free to play with starter decks. There are no ads, no accounts and no time limits, and it works offline.

Party Pass: an optional, one-time purchase that unlocks every deck in the app, including all future decks. It is not a subscription: you pay once, nothing renews, and there is nothing to cancel. The price is shown in your local currency in the app before you buy.

Restore purchases: on a new or reset iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account, then open the app → Settings (gear icon) → Restore Purchases. You can also tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Party Pass screen.

Family Sharing: Party Pass supports Apple Family Sharing. When one family organizer or member buys it, up to five other people in the same Family Sharing group get it too at no extra cost. If it doesn't appear on a family member's device, have them open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026. This policy explains how the iOS app “Imposter Game: Spy Party Games” (“the app”), published by Snir Tam, handles information.

The short version

We don't collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no notifications. Everything you type stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Player names you enter, so your group is remembered next time.
  • Game settings and progress, such as chosen decks, which cards you've already seen, and whether onboarding is complete.
  • Your 18+ preference for showing spicy decks.

This information is stored only on your device using standard iOS storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Party Pass is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, including purchases shared through Family Sharing. We never see your name, email, payment details or Apple Account. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that you own Party Pass, so it can unlock content.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Spicy decks are hidden unless a user confirms they are 18 or older.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. You can remove all locally stored information at any time by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
